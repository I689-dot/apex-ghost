# GridCorsa Should Own the GT3 On-Ramp

GridCorsa grows fastest if it stops looking like a cheaper ACC and becomes the easiest way into a real GT3 race weekend. That means real circuits, pit stops, tyres and Safety Rating on a phone, a gamepad or a modest PC, with bots on every grid, no wheel needed and nothing lost if the servers go away. The opening is real and well timed. **EA shut down Real Racing 3 (delisted December 2025, servers off March 2026) and announced no successor.** GT3 is the favourite discipline of about **three in four sim racers**. And the core sims have moved upmarket: only **7.5%** of r/simracing respondents now run setups under $500. For money, the evidence points away from a coin economy that gates cars or progress. That model sank Rennsport's free-to-play launch and got GT7 review-bombed. What works instead is a mix of a paid unlock or yearly club pass, a non-expiring supporter pack and cosmetic-only coins with real-money prices shown. At small scale a 1-5% payer rate earns very little, and EU rules (CPC 2025 principles, the November 2026 Digital Fairness Act proposal) are tightening around exactly the patterns coin stores rely on. For players, the levers that work at a population of tens are things that never need a full lobby: a weekly time-trial with ghosts and friends' times, a few fixed official race slots a day filled by bots, and seasons with cosmetic rewards. Two of GridCorsa's current choices cut against the evidence: the sign-in wall before the menu, and English only. Both can be softened without reversing them. Steam and Google Play matter most for distribution, and each big launch moment comes only once, so build the Play closed test, pre-registration and a Steam page now, and go to Next Fest only with thousands of wishlists already in hand.

## Real Racing 3's empty slot is GridCorsa's opening

The premium GT3 market is crowded and small. Steam averages in 2026 put **ACC at about 2,000-2,500 concurrent players** ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/apps/805550)). Le Mans Ultimate sits in the low thousands, with spikes to **11,941 during its 24h event** ([Boxthislap](https://boxthislap.org/le-mans-ultimate-breaks-steam-player-record-during-24h/)). Assetto Corsa EVO fell about 96% from a launch peak near 25,000 to a few hundred ([Drifted](https://www.drifted.com/most-played-drifting-games-on-steam-july-2025/); [GameCharts](https://gamecharts.org/steam/assetto-corsa-evo)). Rennsport runs in the tens ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/2077750/charts)). Every one of these titles sells on licensed fidelity, wheel feel and paid content. ACC with all DLC costs about **$170** ([SimRacingSetup](https://simracingsetup.com/assetto-corsa/assetto-corsa-competizione-dlc-guide/)), and iRacing moved to **$124.50 a year** in August 2026 before paid cars and tracks ([iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/iracing-subscription-pricing-update-july-2026/)). A solo developer with three circuits and ten invented cars cannot win that contest. Trying to would mean being judged by wheel owners whose rig spending keeps rising: **59.5% of r/simracing respondents now own setups costing $2,000 or more**, against 29.4% in 2022 ([r/simracing survey analysis](https://v0-simracingresults.vercel.app/)).

The underserved space sits below that tier and across devices. EA delisted Real Racing 3, disabled its purchases and switched off its servers on 20 March 2026. It had no offline mode, so players lost everything, and no successor was announced ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/12/ea-is-delisting-real-racing-3-from-the-app-stores-with-servers-shut-down-planned-for-2026); [MobileMatters](https://mobilematters.gg/news/gaming/real-racing-3-is-being-shut-down-what-happens-next)). Mobile racing revenue now goes to arcade and casual games like Pocket Champs, Asphalt Legends and CarX Street ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-arcade%20racing-revenue-us-60412396241bc16eb84404d2)). The only realistic circuit racers left on phones are NetEase's big-budget Racing Master ([Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/racing-master-brings-authentic-racing-action-and-best-in-class-graphics-to-mobile-and-other-platforms)) and the indie Gran Velocita, which is making the same mobile-to-PC move as GridCorsa ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/gran-velocita-real-driving-goes-pc)). Mobile racing still has reach: in 2023 it was the only mobile genre whose downloads grew, to about **1.6B installs** ([GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/04/08/state-of-mobile-gaming-2024-sensor-tower-report)). But it monetises badly, with racing IAP revenue down **11.4% in 2025** ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/games-revenue-growth-stalls-in-2025-as-strategy-emerges-as-the-fastest-growing-genre)). That shapes everything below: Android brings volume and fills grids, while PC buyers and committed regulars bring most of the money.

Going brand-free costs less than it seems and buys something licensed sims can't have. BeamNG.drive, built entirely on invented makes, averaged **20,660 concurrent players on Steam in July 2026**, close to Forza Horizon 6's roughly 29,000 ([The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/an-obscure-physics-sim-is-reeling-in-almost-as-many-players-as-forza-horizon-6-on-steam)). CarX Street, with lookalike cars, passed **50M mobile downloads** and launched in Steam's global top 10 by revenue at $19.99 ([WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-45242)). Manufacturers restrict and approve damage models, and games often scale every car down to the strictest brand. Invented cars can lose their wings and bumpers freely ([RacingGames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/article/the-real-reason-racing-games-ditch-licensed-cars-for-fictional-brands)). The honest counterweight is that the GT3 sim leaders all sell official series licences, and no study isolates licensing as a cause either way. Hardcore buyers who want a specific 992 GT3 R will remain hard to win. The positioning that follows from the evidence is a single promise: **the full GT3 race weekend on any device, a wheel optional, never an empty grid, and your progress keeps working offline.** Each part answers a documented complaint about a rival: the DLC bill, the hardware cost, empty lobbies in small sims, and RR3's bricked purchases. One risk is unresolved: no research checked whether using real circuit names commercially needs permission. Settle that before the paid launch.

## Sell access and looks, never pace or progress

Sim racers have shown clearly what they will pay for. They pay for content: Le Mans Ultimate sold **520,000 copies plus 1.5 million DLC items**, about three per owner ([Traxion](https://traxion.gg/?p=109977)). They pay for organised competition: LMU's RaceControl subscription passed **40,000 paid subscribers**, around 18% of company revenue in H1 2026 and roughly a 7.7% attach rate on owners ([Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/13/msgm-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/); [BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/companies/motorsport-games-inc/pulses/69735)). They pay for cosmetics, as Rocket League's visible-item Blueprints show ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2019/12/the-big-rocket-league-update-that-kicked-out-loot-boxes-is-now-live/page=1/)). They reject a currency that gates car access. Rennsport abandoned its battle pass and car-purchase credits in August 2025, saying players want "predictability, polish, and permanence, not seasons full of gated content", and it kept leftover RENN$ for cosmetics only ([Rennsport.gg](https://www.rennsport.gg/rennsport-has-taken-on-your-feedback-reworked-pricing-model/)). GT7 priced some cars at up to 8x their GT Sport cost and then cut race payouts. It became Sony's lowest user-rated game on Metacritic until Polyphony apologised and gave every player 1M credits ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/following-microtransaction-criticism-gran-turismo-7s-latest-patch-makes-it-harder-to-earn-cars); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/gran-turismo-7-is-still-being-review-bombed-by-angry-fans)). On mobile, pay-to-win and monetisation drive **28% of negative reviews** for sports and racing games, second only to bugs ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/gaming-app-reviews-what-mobile-gamers-hate-most-2026)).

The arithmetic favours charging for access over relying on coins. Public all-genre benchmarks put lifetime payer conversion at about 1-5% and daily conversion at **0.4% at the median** ([AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/blog/mobile-game-kpis); [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arppu-mobile-games)). Racing has historically had below-average ARPDAU ([GameAnalytics 2018](https://tap-nation.io/assets/docs/Mobile_Benchmarks_Industry_Report_2018_GameAnalytics.pdf)). As an illustration from those ranges: 10,000 installs at 2% payers and a vendor-cited D90 ARPPU of $7-10 comes to roughly **$1,400-2,000** before platform fees. Coins alone can't fund a developer at GridCorsa's likely scale. A one-time unlock or yearly pass earns far more per committed player, and Trackmania's free Starter tier plus **$19.99-a-year Club Access** shows a lighter racing game can do it ([Trackmania.com](https://www.trackmania.com/news/956)). RaceRoom shows the matching acquisition tactic. Its everything-free month drew **260,000 players and 5.5M sessions**, followed by a sale of up to 50% off ([Traxion](https://traxion.gg/raceroom-sets-new-record-during-free-to-play-promotion/); [Online Race Driver](https://www.onlineracedriver.com/2024/06/19/raceroom-free-access-weekend-and-2024-summer-sale)).

The model that fits GridCorsa has four layers. The first is a generous free tier: every car, at least one circuit, online racing and the time-trial, enough that free players fill grids for paying ones. The second is a one-time "Full Grid" unlock or a yearly Club pass in the $10-20 range covering all circuits, career and championships, ranked seasons and special events. This maps GridCorsa's existing SR, licence and Driver Rating systems onto the RaceControl pattern rather than onto a coin sink. The third is a non-expiring supporter pack at $4.99 with coins, an exclusive livery and a badge. The fourth is cosmetic-only coins for paints, finishes, rims, helmets and suits. All GT3 cars stay earnable or free under balance of performance, because the EU Digital Fairness Act proposal would ban pay-to-win and pay-to-progress in games directed at minors ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)). One nuance from the review evidence: Rennsport was criticised specifically for paid liveries ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/19665/-/reviews)). So the paint, number and livery editor itself must be free, and coins should buy extras on top of it, never the ability to look like yourself.

### Compliance turns the coin store into a design spec

GridCorsa's design has no loot boxes and sells cosmetics, which keeps it low-risk. The rules still decide the store's details. The EU CPC Network's principles (March 2025) cover any currency bought with real money. They require showing the real-money value of both coins and items, ban pack sizes that force leftover currency, and require plain terms and protection for minors ([Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2025/03/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper)). The DFA proposal would make real-money display mandatory, switch paid currency off by default in games rated under 18, and blacklist persistent scarcity messaging and confirmshaming. Fines could reach **6% of global turnover**. It is still a proposal and will be amended ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)). In the US, the FTC's **$20M HoYoverse settlement** requires that items sold for virtual currency also be purchasable directly in dollars ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)). PEGI's June 2026 criteria raise the age rating to at least 12 for time-limited offers and battle passes, and for login streaks that take progress away when a day is missed ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/games-loot-boxes-get-16-plus-age-rating-europe-under-pegi-overhaul)). Put together, the store should show a money price beside every coin price, match pack sizes to item prices or allow direct purchase, run no countdown timers, avoid "no thanks, I like losing" copy, publish a refund policy for unused coins, and include parental gating. Promo codes and referrals sit outside the loot-box rules.

### Every storefront takes a different cut and sets different rules

Google moved the US, UK and EEA to a split service fee plus a **5% Play Billing fee** from 30 June 2026. Google's help page and PocketGamer.biz disagree on the first-$1M rate, so check the live page before setting prices ([Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/google-play-expands-billing-options-and-lowers-fees-for-developers/)). Steam requires every in-game purchase in the Steam build to go through the Steam Wallet at a **30% share up to $10M** ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/valves-new-revenue-sharing-favours-big-budget-games-and-indie-devs-arent-happy)). Whether coins bought on Android can be spent in a Steam build is unconfirmed. Rennsport keeps paid entitlements per platform for this reason ([Rennsport.gg](https://www.rennsport.gg/rennsport-has-taken-on-your-feedback-reworked-pricing-model/)). Ask Valve before promising cross-platform wallets. For the existing direct PC download, a merchant of record such as Paddle (**5% + $0.50**) handles VAT and sales tax, which Stripe Tax leaves with the seller ([G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/paddle-vs-xsolla); [AlexCloudstar](https://alexcloudstar.com/md/blog/merchant-of-record-indie-saas-2026/)). The fixed $0.50 takes about half of a $0.99 pack, so direct checkout should start at $4.99. Epic's store lets developers keep **100% of the first $1M per product per year** ([Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/news/epic-games-store-updates-revenue-share-keep-100-of-the-first-1m-per-product-per-year)). That makes it a cheap second PC storefront, though not a discovery engine. For creators, Epic's Support-A-Creator template pays **5% of attributed spend** with mandatory disclosure ([Epic SAC](https://sac.epicgames.com/overview/?lang=zh-CN)), and it fits GridCorsa's existing referral and promo-code system directly. Avoid paid-entry events with cash prizes, which can count as gambling in some jurisdictions. That risk was not researched in depth.

## Steam and Play reward games that arrive with an audience

Steam is close to mandatory for PC reach, and it got harder in 2026. In June Valve reportedly raised the "Popular Upcoming" threshold from about **7,000 to about 100,000 wishlists** and pointed niche games to the per-player Personal Calendar, which recommends titles for two months either side of launch ([PCGuide](https://www.pcguide.com/news/steams-new-100000-wishlist-rule-means-many-indie-devs-will-have-to-rely-on-a-different-feature-to-be-discovered/)). At the February 2026 Next Fest the median demo gained only **about 200 wishlists** among 3,500+ entrants, and gains went mostly to games that arrived with existing interest ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/who-won-february-2026s-steam-next)). A game can enter only one Next Fest ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest)). Wishlists convert to first-week sales at a median **0.15x, falling to 0.10x above $10**. Games that beat that spent **214 days** on Steam before release, against 411 for underperformers, and had 91% early review scores against 67% ([GameDiscoverCo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam)). The floor for small racers is sobering. A solo-developed supercar Early Access game peaked at **19 concurrent players** ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Free-on-Steam-The-first-real-MMO-racing-game-back-again-now-with-supercars.1195681.0.html)), and the polished indie Inertial Drift peaked at 240 ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/1184480/charts)).

Whether to launch on Steam as paid or free-to-play is the hardest call in this report. Chris Zukowski's advice is not to go free. Steam's widgets weight revenue, F2P games get one wishlist email and no sale notifications, and switching from free to paid later wipes the reviews ([How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/07/29/dont-make-your-game-free/)). But that advice targets single-player indies. Slapshot Rebound, a multiplayer F2P game, reached **600-650k licences with $0 paid marketing**, earning half its revenue from cosmetics and $15 "lifetime premium" packs ([GameDiscoverCo, 2021](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/deep-dive-analyzing-slapshot-rebounds)). GridCorsa can take the best of both because crossplay pools all platforms. On Steam, a low-priced paid game ($9.99-14.99, with a free demo) gets revenue-weighted visibility and discount notifications. Android and the web supply the free players who fill grids. This is my recommendation, not a sourced finding. The F2P route stays valid if the Android funnel turns out too thin to fill Steam lobbies. At GridCorsa's content level the price has to stay low: Rennsport was punished for charging full price for 18 cars and 13 tracks and has since cut prices repeatedly, including a permanent 50% cut to Standard ([Boxthislap](https://boxthislap.org/rennsport-launches-touring-classics-part-1-and-a-50-price-cut/)).

On Android, the 1.6 GB build fits Google Play with Play Asset Delivery: a base module up to 500 MB, asset packs up to 1.5 GB each, and up to 4 GB delivered at install time. Extra circuits can download as fast-follow packs, which also cuts the first download ([Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859372)). A personal developer account created after November 2023 must run a closed test with **12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days** before production access ([Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)). That is the first gating task, and Discord plus the referral list can supply the testers. Pre-registration converted about **50% of sign-ups to installs** for Nexon, and a pre-registration reward raised that by 20% (2019). The reward must exist as an in-app product before pre-registration opens ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/console/about/nexon-casestudy/); [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2019/08/nexon-increases-day-60-retention-and.html?hl=fi)), and an existing promo-code livery suits it. Racing has the **highest installs per thousand ad impressions in gaming (16.2)** ([Adjust via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/adjust-gaming-app-insights-report)). Published Android CPIs range from $0.05 to $2.50 depending on geography ([Singular](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/singular-mobile-market-trends-in); [Mapendo](https://mapendo.co/blog/mobile-games-cpi-2025)). A $100-300 test in cheap regions could seed lobbies, but scaled paid acquisition makes sense only once D7 retention and coin revenue are measured.

Web portals give huge reach on tight limits. Poki claims **100M+ monthly players**, and **62% of web gamers say they have bought or downloaded a game they found on the web** ([Poki 2026 State of Web Gaming](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-06-25-the_2026_state_of_web_gaming_report_4ee6fdc260_wp/)). Poki's exclusive deal covers only the open web, Discord and YouTube Playables, leaving Steam and the app stores free ([Poki](https://developers.poki.com/guide/revenue-deal-types)). CrazyGames caps the initial download at **50 MB** and total size at 250 MB, and doesn't list Godot as a documented engine ([CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/)). A one-circuit "GridCorsa Lite" with calls to action pointing to Steam and Play is a credible later-stage funnel. It conflicts with the current Godot-only scope and the 1.6 GB build, though, so it stays optional.

Creators and the community multiply everything else. Big sim creators like Jimmy Broadbent (900k+ subscribers) centre on iRacing, ACC and Automobilista ([Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Broadbent)). Traxion and OverTake regularly run indie racing roundups ([Traxion](https://traxion.gg/six-must-play-indie-racing-games-still-to-come-in-2026/); [OverTake](https://overtake.gg/news/comments/63707)), so start there with a press kit, a demo and a per-creator referral code. That code gives attribution no key-distribution platform offers. Slapshot's single Polish streamer push (about 64k concurrent Twitch viewers) produced its daily-active peak ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/deep-dive-analyzing-slapshot-rebounds)), so a "race the streamer's viewers in a 12-car room" hook is worth pitching. r/simracing restricts self-promotion and asks developers to message moderators first ([r/simracing mirror](https://r-weld.vercel.app/r/simracing)). Referral programmes typically reach a viral coefficient of **0.3-0.7** ([GrowSurf](https://growsurf.com/statistics/mobile-app-referral-statistics/), vendor data). Even 0.3 multiplies every other channel by about 1.4x, provided the referrer is paid only after the invitee completes a milestone and the invite deep-links into the inviter's room. One reach limit is self-imposed: English is the primary language of only **33% of Steam users**, level with Simplified Chinese at 33.6% ([GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/08/29/every-day-more-than-100000-new-buyers-appear-on-steam-and-other-figures-from-valves-report-at-gamescom-2025)). Keeping the game English-only is a valid choice. Localizing just the store page into Simplified Chinese, German, Russian and Portuguese is a cheap middle step to consider.

## Async leaderboards and fixed race times beat empty lobbies

Expect retention to be low and judge it against the right benchmarks. GameAnalytics' 2026 report (2025 data) puts mobile medians at **22% D1, just under 4% D7 and about 0.7% D30**, with the top quartile at 30% / 6-7% / 1.6-1.8%. PC medians are **7% / 1.2% / 0.2%** and the top quartile reaches 12-13% / about 3% / about 0.8% ([GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks)). The one racing-specific Android figure, from a vendor blog with no stated method, is about 20% / 4% / 0.7% ([Asohot](https://www.asohot.com/blog/mobile-game-retention-rate-strategies/)). iOS beats Android by 5-8 points of D1 in every dataset, and GridCorsa ships only on Android. Sessions differ by platform too: about 3 minutes on mobile against 18 on PC. So the phone loop should be sprints and time-trial attempts, and the PC loop full qualifying plus race.

| Metric | Android median | Android top quartile (target) | PC median | PC top quartile (target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | ~20-22% | ~25-30% | ~7% | 12-13% |
| D7 | ~4% | 6-7% | ~1.2% | ~3% |
| D30 | ~0.7% | ~1.7% | ~0.2% | ~0.8% |

*Sources: GameAnalytics 2026; Asohot (racing Android, directional). Android top-quartile D1 is 25-27% per [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks).*

The biggest drop happens on install day ([Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-onboarding-retention)), which puts GridCorsa's sign-in-before-the-menu rule directly in the way. The evidence against a forced sign-in wall is practitioner guidance plus e-commerce data, not a racing A/B test. It all points one way: create a guest account silently, keep progress, and ask for an upgrade at moments of value such as a purchase, joining friends or switching device ([Supercraft](https://supercraft.host/wiki/game-server-backend/guest_login_and_account_upgrade); [Descope](https://www.descope.com/blog/post/gaming-auth); [Enclave Games](https://enclavegames.com/p/ask-players-register.html)). A one-tap device or guest identity created on first launch keeps the rule that every player is signed in before the menu, while removing the form. The user should decide this from GridCorsa's own funnel data: app open, sign-in shown, signed in, first lap, first race, first online race, D1 return. The first session should follow the Forza pattern. The player is driving within minutes (Forza Horizon 3 took about three; [Business Insider NL](https://www.businessinsider.nl/forza-horizon-3-opening-2016-9)), assists are picked automatically for touch, pad or wheel, a short chase-the-ghost hot lap comes first, then a bot race the player narrowly wins, then a reward. Ranked online opens after a short licence test, the way GT7 gates Sport Mode behind single-player progress ([racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/article/gran-turismo-7-daily-races-a-guide-to-this-week-gt7-races)).

Every racing live game that works runs a predictable rhythm of fresh competition, and the cheapest version needs no other players online. Trackmania refreshes its Track of the Day leaderboard every 24 hours ([Trackmania docs](https://doc.trackmania.com/play/what-is-totd/)). Its free Weekly Shorts give newcomers five short tracks every Sunday ([Trackmania blog](https://blog.trackmania.com/?p=8560)). GT7 has rotated three Daily Races every Monday for its whole life, mostly by changing rules (fuel and tyre multipliers, fixed setups, rating toggles) rather than adding assets ([GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/?p=138089)). For GridCorsa that means a **weekly time-trial**: one circuit and car, global and friends boards, downloadable ghosts. It works at any population and should be the main competitive product. On top of it go **3-4 official race slots a day** with a countdown and a "drivers registered" count, filled to 12 with bots so they always start on time. Trackmania moved Cup of the Day to three times a day for time zones ([Trackmania wiki mirror](https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/TrackMania_Nations_(2020_video_game))). Battlefield 6 players report what breaks when backfill doesn't guarantee a start ([EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/idea/battlefield-6-bug-reports-en/matchmaking-not-filling-small-modes-with-ai-to-start-the-round-bot-quota-issue/12808994)). Keep a single pool across PC, Android and browser, and resist splitting queues. Rocket League closed casual extra modes for exactly that reason ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/rocket-league-ranks-what-to-know-for-season-3)). Real Racing 3's best idea is worth borrowing: AI opponents built from friends' recorded laps ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/real-racing-3/real-racing-3s-asynchronous-multiplayer-turns-ghosts-into-ai-competitors)). With GridCorsa's ghost and bot systems, that turns empty seats into real rivals. Seasons of 8-12 weeks with a soft Driver Rating reset and cosmetic rewards (liveries, rims, numbers) follow Rocket League's model ([Epic help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/en-US/rocket-league-c5719357623323/gameplay-c7262179951387/what-are-rocket-league-season-rewards-a5720149751579)). Three circuits mean the rotation repeats every three weeks, so variety has to come from car-and-track pairings, weather, time of day, reverse layouts and rule twists.

The churn evidence in sim racing centres on fairness, not content. Fear of losing rating "takes the fun out" in ACC ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/805550/discussions/0/4339861336967383891)). LMU drivers complain of being penalised when they were the ones hit ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2399420/discussions/0/594032586879410936/)). An iRacing study of 19,000+ races found rage-quitting is contagious and that racing familiar, same-region drivers changes persistence ([ScholarSpace](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/996a0d84-fc62-49cf-ad9c-68ba77da5c16)). GT7 runs races with Driver Rating or Safety Rating switched off and trialled fully rating-free races for newcomers ([GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-7-daily-races-20250922)). For GridCorsa, the priorities in order are: contact attribution so the car that was hit keeps its SR; a Rookie lane with SR on and DR off; a no-stakes race option; SR penalties that scale with severity and fade; and reporting with clip capture once replays exist. Friends leaderboards are "the original and best social feature" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/leaderboards-the-original-and-best-social-feature-)), and "X beat your time at Spa" is the strongest return trigger GridCorsa can send. Avoid daily-login chains. Research on "daily pests" finds missed streaks breed frustration ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365003534_Daily_Quests_or_Daily_Pests_The_Benefits_and_Pitfalls_of_Engagement_Rewards_in_Games)), PEGI raises the age rating when a missed day costs progress, and the DFA would let players switch such features off. A weekly "complete three races" goal that never resets progress gets the habit without the penalty.

## Reviews are won on frame rate, bots and force feedback, not car count

Recent launches show what players actually judge. Project Motor Racing launched "Very Negative" over handling, force feedback, CPU bottlenecks and weak AI, and later fixes lifted only its recent reviews, not its overall score ([Operation Sports](https://www.operationsports.com/project-motor-racing-launches-to-criticism-as-developers-outline-fixes/); [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/project-motor-racings-2-0-update-long-road-to-redemption/)). Rennsport's reviews named AI that was either "suicidally reckless" or "unfathomably slow", along with low frame rates and thin content for the price ([Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/gaming/rennsport-review-light-content-and-plagued-bugs)). AC EVO angered its community by dropping its promised career mode ([Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/assetto-corsa-evo-rolls-out-update-05-amid-career-mode-controversy-1674123/)). Forza Motorsport's per-car levelling grind was one of Turn 10's own "top three" complaints, and it had to be removed ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/turn-10-promises-big-changes-to-address-top-three-forza-motorsport-complaints-rides-rules-and-racers)). In polls, adaptive, human-like AI tops career wishes at **64.2%** ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/what-sim-racers-really-want-from-a-single-player-career-mode-experience.3481/)). Rennsport's own survey ranked AI, wheel compatibility and stability above more content ([Boxthislap](https://boxthislap.org/rennsport-prioritizes-ai-force-feedback-stability-next-development-phase/)). For a game whose grids are mostly bots, AI quality *is* the multiplayer experience. The launch checklist follows from this: smooth frame rates on modest PCs and mid-range phones, bots that neither dive-bomb nor crawl, out-of-the-box force feedback presets for the brands that dominate (**Moza 24.1%, Fanatec 21.7%, Logitech 17.9%**; [r/simracing survey](https://v0-simracingresults.vercel.app/)), a working career and championships, and never announcing a feature that later gets cut. First impressions on Steam are close to permanent, so an Early Access launch with a clearly stated scope is safer than a big-bang release.

GridCorsa can generate word of mouth in places licensed sims can't. BeamNG's destruction physics and Wreckfest's tribute cars show damage itself is the shareable moment ([The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/an-obscure-physics-sim-is-reeling-in-almost-as-many-players-as-forza-horizon-6-on-steam)). Invented cars can shed parts freely, so a replay camera with one-key "save the last 30 seconds", a photo mode and a lap-card image ("1:58.3 at Spa, P4 this week") turn crashes and close finishes into free marketing. Livery creation is the second lever. LMU's custom liveries, its livery competition whose winner went into the game, and a later sharing hub all generated community content ([LMU](https://lemansultimate.com/creating-custom-liveries); [Dakins Design](https://www.dakinsdesign.com/news/lmu-official-artwork)). Car customisation and livery editors are top-five wishes in OverTake's poll at 17.9% and 13.8% ([OverTake](https://www.overtake.gg/news/ai-career-damage-what-the-overtake-community-wants-to-improve-most-in-sim-racing.2884/)). Sharing designs by code, visible to every driver in a room, also makes up for missing brands: give each invented car a clear archetype and let players make it theirs. Modding is the proven longevity engine. The 2014 Assetto Corsa still has **about twelve times EVO's players** and set a record 20,019 concurrent in December 2025 ([Boxthislap](https://boxthislap.org/assetto-corsa-more-players-than-evo/); [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/assetto-corsa-breaks-concurrent-player-records-nearly-10-years-after-release/)). For a solo developer it is the long-term answer to "only three tracks", reached in stages: open liveries first, presets and league-room rules next, tracks last. The hardcore segment's VR (28%) and ultrawide (32%) adoption ([r/simracing survey](https://v0-simracingresults.vercel.app/)) matters only if wheel users become a primary target. Under the on-ramp positioning, VR belongs on the stated roadmap rather than in the launch scope. Cheap accessibility wins still count: colour-blind-safe flags and racing line, full remapping, and assist defaults chosen by input device, the benchmark Forza Motorsport set ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/forza-motorsport-2023-is-taking-accessibility-seriously-with-blind-driving-assists)).

## A twelve-month plan one developer can run

The order matters more than any single step. Each launch moment (the Play launch with auto-install, the single Next Fest, the Steam release) happens once, so each has to arrive with interest already built. Retention also has to be measured before money goes into ads. The plan below orders work by return per hour of solo-developer effort, and it keeps live ops as server-side data (a JSON calendar of events pushed weeks ahead) rather than app releases.

| When | Action | Why it comes first |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 0-4 | Add funnel analytics (open, sign-in shown, signed in, first lap, first race, first online race, D1/D7) | Every later decision needs these numbers; install day is the biggest drop |
| Weeks 0-4 | Decide on one-tap guest/device sign-in that satisfies "signed in before the menu" | Removes the strongest documented first-session friction without dropping the rule |
| Weeks 0-4 | Start the Google Play closed test (12+ testers, 14 days) from Discord and referrals; split the AAB into Play Asset Delivery packs | Gates production access; smaller first download |
| Weeks 0-6 | Ship the weekly time-trial: friends tab, ghosts, "beaten" notifications, Monday rotation | Works at any population; strongest cheap retention loop |
| Weeks 2-8 | Official race slots 3-4 times a day, guaranteed bot backfill, registered-driver count, single crossplay pool | Concentrates thin populations; never an empty grid |
| Weeks 2-8 | SR fairness pass: contact attribution, Rookie lane (DR off), no-stakes races, fading penalties | Fairness drives sim-racing churn more than content does |
| Weeks 4-10 | Coin store to spec: real-money prices shown, matched packs, no timers, refund policy, parental gating; $4.99 supporter pack; Play Billing | Turns on revenue without the Rennsport/GT7 backlash or EU/FTC exposure |
| Weeks 6-12 | Play pre-registration with a promo-code livery reward, then production launch | About half of pre-registrations convert; reward must exist first |
| Weeks 6-12 | Steam Coming Soon page with a gameplay-first trailer; Discord webhook for results | Starts the wishlist build-up about 6-9 months before release |
| Months 3-5 | Replay clip export, photo mode, lap cards; free livery editor with share codes | Damage plus sharing is GridCorsa's word-of-mouth engine |
| Months 3-6 | Per-creator referral codes (5% of attributed spend); pitch Traxion and OverTake; streamer-vs-viewers rooms; milestone-gated referral rewards | Low-cost reach with first-party attribution |
| Months 4-6 | First season (8-12 weeks): soft DR reset, cosmetic rewards, championship standings | Recurring reason to return; feeds the Club pass |
| Months 5-8 | Introduce the Full Grid unlock or $10-20/yr Club pass (all circuits, ranked seasons, events) | The highest-yield model at small scale, per sim-racing precedent |
| Months 6-9 | Steam demo into one Next Fest only with 2-5k+ wishlists; Steam Early Access at $9.99-14.99, scope stated | Next Fest rewards existing interest; low price fits the content |
| Ongoing | A new car or layout every 1-2 months; monthly special event (night, rain, endurance, one-make) | A cadence one developer can sustain |
| After D7 data | A $100-300 Android ad test in cheap regions; scale only if retention and ARPPU justify it | Paid acquisition before retention data burns money |
| Optional | Localized store pages; a sub-50 MB web Lite build for Poki or CrazyGames; VR | Extra reach, each with scope trade-offs to weigh |

A few results would change this plan. If D1 on Android stays well under 20% after onboarding fixes, the problem is the first session, not acquisition, and spending on reach should pause. If Steam wishlists stall below about 2,000 a month before the planned Next Fest, delay the Fest rather than spend the one entry. If crossplay lobbies stay mostly bots even at the scheduled slots, consider switching Steam to free-to-play on the Slapshot model.

## Conclusion

The deepest finding is that GridCorsa's small scale changes which mechanics matter. Big sims retain players through dense matchmaking and licensed content, and neither is available to a solo developer. The tools that work at a population of tens are asynchronous competition, scheduling and AI, and GridCorsa already owns those: ghosts, bots, SR, Driver Rating and championships. Bot quality has moved from a single-player nicety to the core of the multiplayer product. The weekly leaderboard, not the 12-car room, is the real competitive heart of the game.

The same logic changes the money question. Coins for real money are legal and worth having, but at GridCorsa's likely scale they are a supplement and a compliance liability rather than a business model. Revenue will come from committed players buying access and identity: a pass or unlock, a supporter pack, and their own car's look. That makes trust the asset to protect. Grids that are never empty, progress that survives offline, a store that never hides a price, and a promised feature that never gets cut are what separate GridCorsa from Rennsport, RR3 and EVO in the minds of the players most likely to pay.
