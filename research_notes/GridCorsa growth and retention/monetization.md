# Monetization strategies for an indie cross-platform sim-lite racing game (GridCorsa), 2024-2026

Research date: 2026-11-18. Scope: business models, benchmarks, player acceptance, legal and platform rules for selling coins, creator and esports revenue. Jurisdiction is marked on each legal point. Older data has its year.

## 1. Premium vs F2P vs freemium-with-paid-content vs subscription: what works for racing / sim games

### Takeaway
In sim racing, the models with public evidence of working are the paid base plus paid content (Le Mans Ultimate), a subscription plus paid content (iRacing, and LMU's RaceControl add-on), and a small free tier plus paid content (RaceRoom, Trackmania's yearly Club Access). Sim racers pushed back hard against F2P built on a currency, a battle pass and seasonal gating. Rennsport dropped exactly that model in August 2025 after negative reception, and GT7's grind-plus-credits backlash in 2022 is the classic warning. Arcade/mobile racing (CarX, Asphalt, Real Racing 3) is a different market that runs on F2P IAP, and even a 12-year EA title (RR3) was shut down in 2025-26.

### Inferences
- For a sim-lite GT3 game on PC, the evidence favours: (a) a free entry tier with limited content (a RaceRoom- or Trackmania-style starter), (b) one-time content or full-game unlocks, or a yearly "club" pass in the $10-20 range (Trackmania sets the anchor for a lighter sim), and (c) cosmetics. A currency-gated car economy with a battle pass is the pattern sim racers reject (Rennsport 2025, GT7 2022).
- LMU's RaceControl shows a mid-size sim can add a subscription for organised online racing (ratings, scheduled series) on top of paid content. 40K subs out of about 520K owners is roughly 7.7% (my calculation). GridCorsa's Safety Rating, Driver Rating and championships map onto that kind of paid "competition" tier rather than onto a coin sink.
- RR3's shutdown is a reminder that online-only F2P titles leave buyers with nothing when the servers close. Offline play for bots and career is both a trust signal and a hedge against consumer-law exposure (this is an inference, not a cited legal requirement).
- Rocket League's success came with Epic's backing and an existing 10M+ player base. It is not proof that F2P will work for a solo developer.

### Gaps

- No revenue figures were found for RaceRoom, Trackmania Club Access uptake, Forza Motorsport/Horizon (car passes, VIP), Asphalt Legends Unite or CarX worldwide totals.
- No public conversion data was found for any sim-racing F2P title.

## 2. Benchmarks: conversion, ARPDAU, ARPPU, coin ladders, starter packs, battle passes

### Takeaway
Credible genre-specific racing benchmarks are not public (GameAnalytics and others publish all-genre aggregates; racing-specific data sits behind Sensor Tower/AppMagic paywalls). Working ranges are 1-5% payer conversion over the lifetime of a typical F2P game, and daily conversion of about 0.4% at the median and about 1.2% in the top 15%. Racing has historically had below-average ARPDAU (2018 data). Retention is the binding constraint: median mobile D7 is just under 4%, and PC D1 in the top quartile is only 15-16%.

### Cited Findings
- GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks (2025 data; 16,262 mobile games and 3,582 PC games). Mobile D7: median just under 4%, top 25% 6-7%, top 10% 11-12%. Mobile D30: median 0.69-0.79%. PC D1: top 25% 15-16%. PC D30: top 10% about 2.5%. PC median daily playtime is 32-33 min and median PC DAU/MAU is 4-5%. The report has no genre split and no conversion or ARPDAU data. — [GameDevReports summary of GameAnalytics 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)
- The 2025 GameAnalytics report covered 11,600 mobile games, 16 genres and 2024 data. — [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks); [PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-GameAnalytics-Mobile-Gaming-Benchmarks.pdf)
- Daily conversion (GameAnalytics-sourced): about 1.2% for the 85th percentile, 0.4% median and 0.1% bottom. Genre not stated. — [AppFollow 2026 KPI guide](https://appfollow.io/blog/mobile-game-kpis)
- GameAnalytics 2018: "role playing genre dominates with ARPDAU, whereas arcade and racing games average much less." (2018, qualitative.) — [GameAnalytics 2018 report](https://tap-nation.io/assets/docs/Mobile_Benchmarks_Industry_Report_2018_GameAnalytics.pdf)
- Vendor ranges (lower reliability): payer rate 2-5% for most F2P games and under 1% for hyper-casual; another source gives 1-3%. One cites D90 IAP ARPPU of about $7-10 for mobile games (as of October 2026), and another a median modelled ARPPU of $1.88/month with a top decile of $42.76. — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arppu-mobile-games); [MWM](https://mwm.ai/glossary/mobile-game-monetization); [MetricsTree](https://metricstree.vercel.app/en/blog/arpdau-mobile-games)
- Starter packs: vendor guidance says first purchase is the key conversion, and about every second first-time payer buys again. It suggests A/B-testing $0.99 against $1.99 starter packs, but gives no outcome data. — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arppu-mobile-games); [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-04-02-f2p-monetization-models-comparison-2026/)
- Price anchors in racing: GT7 sold 2M Cr for $19.99 (2022) — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/following-microtransaction-criticism-gran-turismo-7s-latest-patch-makes-it-harder-to-earn-cars); iRacing charges $11.95-14.95 per car or track — [BoxThisLap](https://boxthislap.org/how-does-iracings-pricing-model-work/); Trackmania charges $19.99/yr — [Trackmania.com](https://www.trackmania.com/news/956).
- Season pass / battle pass: Rennsport's battle pass was dropped in 2025 for being seen as gated monetization — [Rennsport.gg](https://www.rennsport.gg/rennsport-has-taken-on-your-feedback-reworked-pricing-model/). Under PEGI's 2026 criteria, battle passes and time-limited offers bring a minimum PEGI 12 (see section 4) — [Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/games-loot-boxes-get-16-plus-age-rating-europe-under-pegi-overhaul).

### Inferences
- With PC median DAU/MAU at 4-5% and median new players at 600-900 a month on PC (GameAnalytics 2026), a small indie F2P game converting 2% of a few thousand MAU makes very little money. A paid unlock or yearly pass usually earns more per player at small scale (an inference from the benchmarks, not a measured comparison).
- A coin ladder should keep the real-money price visible and avoid the "buy more than you need" pattern (see section 4). Practical design: packs priced so that common store items (paint, rims) can be bought in one pack with little leftover, plus a direct real-money price on items.
- The first purchase should be a single, clearly priced, non-expiring "Supporter / Welcome Pack" (for example $2.99-4.99: coins plus an exclusive cosmetic plus a badge). Countdown timers should be avoided because the EU DFA draft blacklists persistent scarcity messaging, and time-limited offers raise the PEGI rating.

### Gaps
- No racing-specific conversion, ARPDAU or ARPPU from GameAnalytics, Sensor Tower or AppMagic was found in public sources (paywalled).
- No public data on battle pass uptake rates (the share of players who buy) for racing games.
- No Deconstructor of Fun or Mobile Dev Memo racing teardown surfaced in searches.

## 3. Cosmetic-only vs pay-to-win vs pay-for-content: what sim racers accept

### Takeaway
Sim racers broadly accept paying for content (cars and tracks, as one-time licences or DLC packs) and for organised competition (subscriptions). They accept cosmetics. They reject anything that touches competitive parity (paid performance), currency-gated car acquisition, inflated grind, and seasonal or FOMO gating. Upcoming EU law will also ban pay-to-win and pay-to-progress in games directed at minors.

### Cited Findings
- iRacing (content licences), LMU (1.5M DLC items) and RaceRoom (paid packs) show paid content is accepted in sim racing. — [BoxThisLap](https://boxthislap.org/how-does-iracings-pricing-model-work/); [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/?p=109977); [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/raceroom-free-content-library-june-20230620/)
- Rennsport's reasons for its reversal included "every racer on the same footing" and no "seasons full of gated content". Leftover RENN$ was limited to cosmetics. — [Rennsport.gg](https://www.rennsport.gg/rennsport-has-taken-on-your-feedback-reworked-pricing-model/)
- Rennsport reviewers named "overpriced cars" and a currency that "forces you to overspend". — [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/rennsport)
- GT7: inflated car prices plus reduced payouts led to review-bombing (2022). — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/gran-turismo-7-is-still-being-review-bombed-by-angry-fans)
- Rocket League monetized cosmetics only, with visible-item Blueprints in place of random crates. — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2019/12/the-big-rocket-league-update-that-kicked-out-loot-boxes-is-now-live/page=1/)
- EU DFA proposal (November 2026), Article 16 / Recital 65: pay-to-win and pay-to-progress prohibited in games directed at minors, with examples such as "artificial difficulty barriers, progression speeds that make the unpaid path illusory, and competitive structures where outcomes are effectively determined by spending". — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)
- Rennsport's items and DLC are tied to the platform they were bought on, while free items work on all platforms. This shows cross-platform entitlement is a known friction. — [Rennsport.gg](https://www.rennsport.gg/rennsport-has-taken-on-your-feedback-reworked-pricing-model/)

### Inferences
- GridCorsa's brand-free cars remove the licensed-car scarcity argument for selling cars. All GT3 cars should probably stay obtainable through play (Balance of Performance parity), with money spent on cosmetics (paints, liveries, numbers, rims, helmets, suits, pit-crew looks), on a supporter pack, and possibly on track packs or an online "club" pass.
- Coins earned in races must not buy performance, and should not turn car access into a grind that coin packs shortcut (the GT7 pattern), or the DFA pay-to-progress concern applies once the PEGI rating is under 18.
- Cross-platform coin and entitlement portability (Android, PC, web) is a selling point, but platform rules complicate it (section 5).

### Gaps
- No survey data quantifying sim-racer acceptance of each monetization type was found. The evidence here is case-based and review-based.

## 4. Ethical design and backlash risks: loot boxes, virtual-currency obfuscation, EU / UK / US regulation, age ratings

### Takeaway
Selling coins is legal everywhere, but the rules tightened in 2025-26. EU regulators (CPC Key Principles, March 2025) expect real-money prices on currency and items, no obscuring exchange rates, no forced surplus currency, clear terms, and protection for minors. The proposed Digital Fairness Act (November 2026) would make real-money price display mandatory, switch paid currency off by default for minors, and ban loot boxes and pay-to-win for minors. The US FTC has fined both Epic and HoYoverse over dark patterns and obscured currency costs. PEGI's June 2026 criteria raise age ratings for paid random items, time-limited offers and login-streak penalties. GridCorsa's current design (no loot boxes, cosmetics priced in coins) is low-risk if coin prices are also shown in real money and packs do not force leftovers.

### Cited Findings
**EU: CPC Network Key Principles on in-game virtual currencies (21 March 2025; soft law)**
- Scope: currencies bought with real money. Currencies earned only through gameplay are excluded. — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/european-consumer-protection-network-issues-new-key-principles-on-in-game-virtual-currencies-impact-for-gaming-and-gambling-entities-in-belgium-the-eu-and-beyond/); [Reed Smith Q&A](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2025/03/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper)
- Principles: show the real-money value of both the currency and the items; no confusing multi-currency systems that hide cost; do not force users to buy more currency than needed; plain-language terms that do not limit consumer rights; respect withdrawal rights (one summary says 14 days for unused currency); protect vulnerable consumers and children. — [Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2025/03/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper); [Sheppard Mullin](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/eu-new-european-consumer-protection-guidelines-for-virtual-currencies-in-video-games); [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/uk/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/1606580/new-european-consumer-protection-guidelines-for-virtual-currencies-in-video-games)
- Industry bodies (EGDF, Video Games Europe) objected on 22 March 2025. A key dispute is whether virtual currency is "digital content" or a "digital representation of value", which affects withdrawal rights. The Commission held a stakeholder workshop in June 2025. — [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news/european-commission-hosts-stakeholders-talks-application-cpc-networks-key-principles-games-virtual-2025-06-03_en); [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/european-consumer-protection-network-issues-new-key-principles-on-in-game-virtual-currencies-impact-for-gaming-and-gambling-entities-in-belgium-the-eu-and-beyond/)
- Non-EU companies targeting EU consumers should follow them. — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/european-consumer-protection-network-issues-new-key-principles-on-in-game-virtual-currencies-impact-for-gaming-and-gambling-entities-in-belgium-the-eu-and-beyond/)

**EU: Digital Fairness Act proposal (tabled about 10-11 November 2026; not yet law)**
- Article 14: traders selling items for their own paid virtual currency must show the real-money equivalent (or an estimate). In games directed at minors (a PEGI rating under 18, per Recital 62), paid virtual currencies are disabled by default and switched on only by a parent. — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)
- Article 15: loot boxes banned in games for minors. In 18+ games, contents and odds must be disclosed and players must be able to switch loot boxes off. Article 16 bans pay-to-win and pay-to-progress for minors. Article 17 gives players the right to switch off compulsive-use features (autoplay, notifications not tied to their activity, login-XP rewards). — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)
- Annex I blacklist: persistent scarcity messaging, pre-ticked paid add-ons, framing a decline as the wrong choice ("confirmshaming"), disproportionately complex refusal steps, and renewal terms shown less prominently than other terms. The CPC Regulation would allow fines of up to 6% of global turnover, and non-EU traders would need an EU legal representative. — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6); [Lovguiden](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/eur-lex-kommissionsforslag/2026-11-11-digital-fairness-act-eu-seeks-to-ban-dark-patterns-and-loot-boxes-for-minors); [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2026/11/11/eu-digital-fairness-act-proposes-major-regulation-on-gaming-lockboxes-subscriptions-and-dark-patterns/)
- Status: Parliament and Council will amend it, so the details may change. — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6); [Freshfields](https://technologyquotient.freshfields.com/post/102ltio/the-eus-proposed-digital-fairness-act-a-game-developers-guide-to-potential-imp)

**Loot boxes by country (EU / UK)**
- Belgium: the gaming regulator has treated paid loot boxes as gambling since 2018 (described as a ban), and an Antwerp court decision (2025) treated real-money loot boxes as unlawful. Netherlands: the KSA treated loot boxes as games of chance, but a Dutch decision was later overturned (details not verified). — [Siege.gg](https://siege.gg/news/several-eu-countries-have-introduced-stricter-regulations-on-loot-boxes-in-games-in-2025); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/direction-of-travel-for-loot-boxes-in-the-eu); [Leon Xiao](https://sites.google.com/view/leon-xiao/policy/belgium)
- UK: there is no gambling-law ban. The 11 Ukie Industry Principles (18 July 2023, DCMS-convened, voluntary) include tech controls so under-18s cannot buy loot boxes without parental consent. — [Lewis Silkin 2023](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2023/07/24/new-uk-principles-and-guidance-on-loot-boxes-published-102ik8a); [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/loot-boxes-in-video-games-update-on-improvements-to-industry-led-protections)
- UK subscription contract rules under the DMCC Act 2024 are due from January 2027. — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/12/come-on-now-play-fair-what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-video-games-102o9e6)

**Age ratings: PEGI criteria (games submitted from June 2026)**
- Paid random items: PEGI 16 by default. Time- or quantity-limited offers, including battle passes and temporary sales: PEGI 12 (7 if spending is parent-locked). Daily quests, login rewards and streaks: at least 7, or 12 if missing a day loses content or progress. Unrestricted chat: 18 unless safeguards exist (reporting, blocking). NFTs: 18. Existing titles are not re-rated. — [Osborne Clarke (8 May 2026)](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/games-loot-boxes-get-16-plus-age-rating-europe-under-pegi-overhaul)

**US: FTC actions**
- Epic/Fortnite: $245M refund order finalised on 14 March 2023 for dark patterns and charges without consent (part of a $520M total with a $275M COPPA penalty). The order bars dark-pattern billing. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/ftc-finalizes-245-million-fine-against-epic-over-i-fortnite-i-sales-practices); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/amp-articles/epic-games-agrees-to-pay-500-million-in-fortnite-ftc-case/1100-6510151/)
- HoYoverse/Genshin Impact (January 2025): $20M penalty. The FTC said the virtual-currency system "serves to obscure the true amount of money that players are spending" and that loot-box odds were misleading. The order bans loot-box sales to under-16s without parental consent, requires disclosure of odds and currency exchange rates, and requires that items sold for virtual currency also be purchasable directly in US dollars. — [FTC business blog](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/ftc-says-genshin-impact-deceived-children-and-orders-its-publisher-to-pay-a-usd20-million-fine-and-stop-selling-loot-boxes-to-kids); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/hoyoverse-agrees-to-20-million-settlement-with-ftc-over-loot-boxes)

### Inferences
- A compliance checklist for GridCorsa's coin store (EU/UK/US):
  - Show a real-money equivalent next to every coin price.
  - Sell packs whose sizes match item prices (no forced leftovers), or let players buy items directly with money.
  - No random paid items.
  - No countdown or scarcity timers.
  - No confirmshaming on "no thanks".
  - A clear refund and withdrawal policy for unused coins (EU 14 days, pending the legal dispute).
  - Spending off by default, or parental gating, if the game will be rated under 18.
  - Reporting and blocking in online chat so it does not trigger an 18 rating.
  - Login-streak rewards that never take away progress.
  - A setting to turn off engagement notifications and streak rewards (anticipating DFA Article 17).
- Keeping coins earn-only on the web build, with any paid coins on Android and PC, keeps the CPC scope clear for the earn-only portion. CPC rules apply as soon as coins can be bought.
- Promotional codes and referrals are not regulated as loot boxes. Referral rewards for minors may still raise "pressure" concerns under the CPC child-protection principle (inference).

### Gaps
- The exact holding and date of the Dutch Raad van State loot-box ruling (2022) was not verified from primary sources.
- The UK CMA's direct consumer-enforcement fines under the DMCC Act (reported as up to 10% of turnover, from April 2025) did not surface in searches and are unverified here.
- Whether the 14-day EU withdrawal right applies to bought currency remains disputed (industry vs CPC).

## 5. Platform rules and costs: Google Play, Steam, direct PC sales (Stripe / Paddle / Xsolla), VAT

### Takeaway
On Android, Google's new fee schedule (from 30 June 2026 in the US, UK and EEA) separates a service fee from a 5% Google Play Billing fee. Alternative billing and external web links avoid the billing fee but still pay a service fee. Other markets keep 15% on the first $1M a year and 30% above, with subscriptions at 15%. On Steam, every in-game purchase must go through Steam Wallet / MicroTxn at a 30% cut until $10M. Off Steam, a merchant of record (Paddle at 5% + $0.50, Xsolla at about 5% plus channel costs) takes on VAT and sales tax; with Stripe and Stripe Tax the tax liability stays with the seller.

### Cited Findings
**Google Play**
- New regional fees (EEA, UK and US from 30 June 2026; Australia and Japan from 30 September 2026, by install date). Auto-renewing subscriptions: 10% + 5% billing fee. Other purchases, standard tier: 20% + 5% for new installs and 25% + 5% for existing installs (20% via external web links). In the Play Games Level Up / Apps Experience programs: 15% + 5% for new and 20% + 5% for existing (15% via web links). The page's table layout was hard to parse, so it should be checked against the live page. — [Google Play Console Help 112622](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en)
- Conflicting secondary report: PocketGamer.biz (25 June 2026) says a 10% service fee applies on the first $1M of annual earnings under all billing methods, and that the reduced 15%/20% rates are for qualifying apps above $1M. No billing fee applies to alternative billing or external links. Developers may build their own billing-choice screens. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/google-play-expands-billing-options-and-lowers-fees-for-developers/). **These conflict with the support-page table on the first-$1M rate. Verify on the live Google page.**
- Markets not yet updated: 15% on the first $1M a year and 30% above, auto-renewing subscriptions 15%, and alternative billing in South Korea and India at 4% less. — [Google Play Console Help 112622](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en)
- EEA external offers: secondary reports cite initial acquisition fees of 5% (subscriptions) and 10% (other) for 2 years, plus ongoing fees of 7% and 17% (unverified). In March 2025 the European Commission said Google's anti-steering limits breached the DMA. — [Developer-Tech](https://www.developer-tech.com/news/google-alters-play-store-developer-fees-rules-in-the-eea/); [Google blog](https://blog.google/around-the-globe/google-europe/an-update-on-google-play-billing-in-the-eea/)

**Steam**
- "You'll need to use the microtransaction API so Steam customers can only make purchases from the Steam Wallet." The Wallet can buy individual items or in-game currency. Refunds trigger automatic claw-back, and GetReport() should be reconciled daily. Recommended: a 72-hour trade lock on new purchases, limits on high-value purchases by new accounts, and avoiding purchases that harm other players' experience. — [Steamworks Microtransactions](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions)
- In-game managed subscriptions can be paid by card, PayPal or Wallet. — [Steamworks recurring billing](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/microtransactions/recurring_billing)
- Revenue share: 30% up to $10M, 25% from $10M to $50M, 20% above (marginal tiers since October 2018). It covers games, DLC, in-game sales and Marketplace fees. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/valves-new-revenue-sharing-favours-big-budget-games-and-indie-devs-arent-happy); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2018/12/01/steam-valve-developer-revenue-agreement/)
- Steam's rules prohibit steering players to other purchase methods (Tim Sweeney's characterisation). Whether currency bought off Steam (for example on mobile) may be spent in the Steam build was not confirmed from official docs. — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/pc-gaming/epic-ceo-tim-sweeney-takes-aim-at-one-of-valves-steam-policies-as-that-massive-uk-lawsuit-looms); [Xsolla D2P + Steam guide](https://xsolla.com/blog/direct-to-player-plus-steam-guide)

**Direct PC / web sales (merchant of record vs processor)**
- Paddle: 5% + $0.50 per transaction, covering checkout, payments, global sales tax and VAT as merchant of record, fraud and support. Lemon Squeezy and Fungies list similar rates (Lemon Squeezy adds 1.5% on non-US sales). — [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/paddle-vs-xsolla); [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/paddle-fees-explained) (competitor-authored)
- Xsolla Pay Station is listed as "5% of transactions + cost of channel" (competitor's reading; verify directly). — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/alternatives/xsolla)
- Stripe is about 2.9% + $0.30 base, plus about 0.5% for Stripe Tax. Stripe Tax calculates the tax, but filing and liability remain with the seller (not a merchant of record). — [dev.to comparison 2026](https://dev.to/jettfu/stripe-vs-paddle-vs-lemon-squeezy-fee-comparison-2026-2c77); [AlexCloudstar](https://alexcloudstar.com/md/blog/merchant-of-record-indie-saas-2026/)
- Note on small purchases: the fixed $0.50 on a $0.99 pack is about 50% of the price. Packs should start at about $4.99 on direct web checkout, or be bundled (my calculation from the stated fee).

### Inferences
- A solo developer selling to EU, UK and US consumers on PC/web should use a merchant of record (Paddle, Xsolla, FastSpring or similar), so that VAT/OSS registration, US sales-tax nexus and chargebacks are handled by the MoR. Stripe alone leaves VAT OSS registration and filing to the developer.
- On Android, Google Play Billing (5% billing fee plus the service fee) is the simple path. A web top-up via an external link saves only the 5% billing fee under the new US/UK/EEA schedule and adds friction.
- If GridCorsa ships on Steam, purchases inside the Steam build must use Steam Wallet. Cross-platform coins (bought on Android, spent on Steam) are a policy grey area to check with Valve. Rennsport keeps paid entitlements per platform for this reason.

### Gaps
- Official Google numbers for the first-$1M service fee under the June 2026 schedule conflict between Google Help and PocketGamer.biz.
- No official Valve statement was found on spending currency bought off Steam inside a Steam build.
- Xsolla's official current rate card was not retrieved.

## 6. Creator and esports revenue: paid leagues, entry fees, sponsorships, creator codes

### Takeaway
Little public evidence was found. The one solid template is Epic's Support-A-Creator: creators get 5% of supporter spend via an in-game creator code, with disclosure required, in Fortnite and Rocket League. In sim racing, organised competition is monetized mainly through subscriptions (iRacing, LMU RaceControl) rather than per-event entry fees.

### Cited Findings
- Epic Support-A-Creator: creators earn 5% (base rate) of supporters' in-game spend in Fortnite and Rocket League, with a minimum of 5% on Epic Store titles. Players enter a creator code in the item shop, the payout threshold is $100, and creators must clearly disclose the relationship. — [Epic SAC](https://sac.epicgames.com/overview/?lang=zh-CN); [Rocket League SAC help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-202300000001622/c-202300000001682/what-is-support-a-creator-for-rocket-league-a202300000015077); [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/22/06/27/epics-support-a-creator-program-pays-out-only-5-of-game-content-makers-sales)
- LMU's RaceControl (paid online competition service) had 40K+ paid subscribers and about $2M in 9-month FY2026 revenue. — [BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/companies/motorsport-games-inc/pulses/69735)
- RaceRoom hosts official DTM, WTCR and GT Masters esports series (a licensing and sponsorship model). — [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/raceroom-free-content-library-june-20230620/)

### Inferences
- For GridCorsa, a creator code (5-10% of attributed coin spend, or a bonus cosmetic for the buyer) fits the existing referral and promo-code system. FTC and EU influencer-disclosure norms mean creators must disclose.
- Paid-entry events with cash prizes risk being classed as gambling or skill-gaming depending on jurisdiction (inference; not researched). A safer route is a paid "club" pass that includes ranked championships, with cosmetic or trophy prizes, or sponsored prize pools.

### Gaps
- No data was found on paid sim-racing league entry fees, league revenue or sponsorship values.
- The legal status of entry-fee tournaments with cash prizes (US state skill-gaming laws, UK Gambling Act, EU member-state rules) was not researched.
