# Competitive Landscape and Market Positioning for an Indie GT3 Sim-Lite (GridCorsa), 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-11-18. Player counts come from third-party Steam trackers (SteamDB, Steambase, Tracker.gg, Raijin, live-player-count, GameCharts). These trackers often disagree, and the conflicts are noted below. Confidence labels: High = primary or official source; Medium = reputable trade press, or two trackers that agree; Low = a single tracker, a blog, or an unverified claim.

## 1. Market size and growth: sim racing and racing games (PC, console, mobile)

### Takeaway
The analyst-firm figures for "sim racing" are not reliable. 2025 estimates run from $0.66B to $1.5B, with CAGRs of 7% to 22%, and most of them include hardware. The audience behind the hard numbers is small but deep-pocketed: a few thousand concurrent players per PC sim on Steam, against hundreds of thousands for mass-market racers like Forza Horizon 6 and F1 25. Mobile racing downloads were growing in 2023-24, but racing IAP revenue fell about 11% in 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Analyst sizing (Low confidence: paywalled, scopes differ, often includes hardware and rigs)**
- Fortune Business Insights values the global racing simulator market at USD 1.19B in 2025 and names esports and organised sim competitions as drivers — [Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/racing-simulator-market-115926)
- LP Information: the sim racing market grows from US$1,528M in 2025 to US$5,072M in 2031 (22.1% CAGR, the most aggressive figure found) — [MarketResearch.com / LP Information](https://www.marketresearch.com/LP-Information-Inc-v4134/Global-Sim-Racing-Growth-Status-40972476)
- The Business Research Company: the racing simulator market reaches $1.24B by 2029 at 7.3% CAGR — [MarketResearch.com / TBRC](https://marketresearch.com/Business-Research-Company-v4006/Racing-Simulator-Global-37935363)
- Another report puts the 2025 market at USD 664.1M, rising to USD 1,657.9M by 2032 (13.96% CAGR) — [GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/ires1971669-racing-simulator-market-by-platform-hardware-type.html)

**Overall games market context (Medium/High)**
- Newzoo's 2025 Global Games Market Report put the market at about $189B, later revised up to about $197B. Shooters remain the top genre, and racing is not among the headline genres — [Outlook Respawn (Newzoo $189B)](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/global-games-market-set-for-189b-in-2025-newzoo-report); [Outlook Respawn (Newzoo raises to $197B)](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/gaming/gaming-news/newzoo-raises-global-games-market-2025-forecast-to-197-billion); [Newzoo free 2025 report PDF](https://nzgda.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2025_Newzoo_Free_Global_Games_Market_Report.pdf)

**Mobile racing (Medium)**
- Sensor Tower: in 2023 racing was the only mobile genre whose downloads grew, up 4% year on year to about 1.6B installs. In Q1 2024 racing downloads were up 11% year on year — [Sensor Tower via gamedevreports / GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/04/08/state-of-mobile-gaming-2024-sensor-tower-report); [Sensor Tower Q1'24 via gamedevreports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-gaming-market-4b7)

- Racing was not in Sensor Tower's top five genres by 2024 mobile IAP revenue. Strategy led at $17.5B, then RPG at $16.8B — [Sensor Tower: Mobile Games in 2024 (gamedevreports)](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-games-in-2024)

**PC/console scale reference points (Medium)**
- Forza Horizon 6 peaked above 300,000 concurrent players on Steam in May 2026 and averaged just under 29,000 in July 2026. BeamNG.drive averaged 20,660 concurrent in July 2026 (peak 47,424), with a "massive gap" to Assetto Corsa in third — [The Drive, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.thedrive.com/news/an-obscure-physics-sim-is-reeling-in-almost-as-many-players-as-forza-horizon-6-on-steam)
- The Gran Turismo series passed 100M units sold (25 June 2025). GT7 has over 2M monthly active users and is "still growing" according to Kazunori Yamauchi (Dec 2025) — [GTPlanet (100M)](https://www.gtplanet.net/gran-turismo-celebrates-100-million-copies-sold-worldwide/); [GTPlanet (2M MAU)](https://www.gtplanet.net/gt7-2-million-monthly-users-20251221/). A claim of 16.5M GT7 copies is unconfirmed — [duPont Registry](https://news.dupontregistry.com/news/nearly-four-years-on-and-gran-turismo-7-continues-to-thrive-closing-out-a-strong-2025/)
- Hardware spend: a GTPlanet community questionnaire (615 responses in 2025) found the share spending under $500 on gear fell from 22.4% (2022) to 10.9% (2025), with nearly half in the $2k-$10k bracket and 7.5% calling themselves beginners. This is a self-selected sample (Low) — [GTPlanet forum](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/sim-racing-trends-2022-vs-2023-vs-2025-questionnaire-analysis.431948/)

### Inferences
- The "core sim" audience is small, ageing upward, and spends more on hardware each year, so the entry tier is thinning. That leaves room for an on-ramp product for players who don't own a wheel or rig.
- Mobile racing has very wide reach (more than 1.6B installs a year) but falling monetisation. Expect it to drive volume and ad revenue, not high ARPU.

### Gaps
- No credible, software-only sizing of sim racing exists in public. Analyst reports are paywalled and mix in hardware.
- No public 2025 racing genre revenue share from Newzoo was found.

## 2. Player counts and business models and prices of the main PC/console competitors

### Takeaway
On Steam the dedicated sims are small: LMU, ACC, AMS2, RaceRoom and iRacing-on-Steam each run in the low thousands at best, AC EVO collapsed after launch, and Rennsport is near zero. The original Assetto Corsa (2014) still beats almost all of them because of mods. Three business models dominate: subscription plus paid content (iRacing), premium plus DLC (ACC, LMU, AC EVO, AMS2, F1), and F2P with a thin free tier and paid content (RaceRoom). Rennsport's failed F2P attempt is a cautionary tale.

### Inferences
- A rough Steam scale ladder for 2025-26 averages: AC (2014) ≫ ACC ≈ LMU (about 2-4k, with spikes to 8-12k at events) > AMS2 ≈ RaceRoom ≈ iRacing-on-Steam (about 1-2k) > AC EVO (about 0.2-1k) > rFactor 2 (about 0.5k) > Rennsport (tens). Steam figures understate iRacing heavily because most of its members run the standalone client.
- GT3 is crowded at the premium end (ACC, LMU, iRacing, AC EVO, RaceRoom). An indie can't win on licensed fidelity, but can win on price of entry and on devices: none of these run on phones, and most need a wheel to be fun.
- The Rennsport story suggests that F2P with weak content and a small player base doesn't hold up, while RaceRoom shows that free weekends and free tiers bring big trial spikes (260k players in a month).
- AC's comeback shows longevity comes from user content and community servers, not launch hype.

### Gaps
- No official 2025 concurrency or MAU figures were found for iRacing, LMU, ACC or Forza Motorsport. No verified F1 25 unit sales were found.
- AMS2's and LMU's current prices were not verified for 2026.

## 3. Mobile and cross-platform racing: competitors, revenue, and the gap for "a real sim on phone and PC with crossplay"

### Takeaway
The leading mobile sim-ish racer, Real Racing 3, was shut down by EA (delisted Dec 2025, servers off 20 Mar 2026), and no successor has been announced. That left the realistic racing slot on mobile without its incumbent. Today's mobile racing revenue goes to arcade and casual titles (Asphalt Legends, CarX Street, Pocket Champs, Hill Climb Racing). No PC-plus-mobile crossplay GT sim with real circuits was found. NetEase's Racing Master is the closest attempt (PC plus mobile, licensed cars, a big-budget Chinese title).

### Cited Findings
- Real Racing 3: EA announced the shutdown on 18 Dec 2025, delisted the game and disabled IAP. Servers closed on 20 Mar 2026 (moved back one day from 19 Mar). The game is unplayable without servers, there is no offline mode, and EA has announced no successor (High/Medium) — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/12/ea-is-delisting-real-racing-3-from-the-app-stores-with-servers-shut-down-planned-for-2026); [GamingonPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/real-racing-3-bids-farewell-in-march-19-2026-after-delivering-12-years-of-unforgettable-memories); [TheTechOutlook](https://www.thetechoutlook.com/new-release/gaming-release/real-racing-3-server-shutdown-extended-by-one-day-new-closure-date-set-for-march-20-2026); [MobileMatters](https://mobilematters.gg/news/gaming/real-racing-3-is-being-shut-down-what-happens-next)
- CarX Street (CarX Technologies): more than 50M mobile downloads and more than $10M mobile revenue in the year before its PC launch. It launched on Steam on 29 Aug 2024 at $19.99 premium with no microtransactions, entered Steam's global top 10 by revenue, and had more than 600k wishlists (Medium, developer claims via trade press). Estimated lifetime Steam gross is $7.2M-$14M (Low) — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-45242); [Drifted](https://drifted.com/carx-street-finally-launches-on-steam); [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/games/1114150). It is also on PS5 — [Nordisk Bil](https://www.nordiskbil.com/carx-street-now-available-also-on-ps5/)
- Sensor Tower country snapshots for CarX Street: US Android weekly revenue was $3.5K-$8K in Q1 2025, and Belarus iOS peaked at $80K/week in May 2025 (Low; directional, AI-generated blog snapshots) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-android-top-5-arcade%20racing-revenue-us-60412396241bc16eb84404d2)
- Asphalt Legends (Unite branding dropped on 24 Jul 2025): one of Gameloft's top five titles, which together made up 57-58% of Gameloft revenue in 2025. US Android weekly revenue was about $22-28K in Q3 2025 (Medium/Low) — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-unity-roblox-good-job-gameloft-and-innogames-numbers-plus-vital-market-stats/); [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-arcade%20racing-revenue-us-60412396241bc16eb84404d2)
- Pocket Champs (Madbox) was the top US mobile racing earner across 2025 snapshots, with peaks of about $88K-$108K per week on US Android (Low/Medium) — [Sensor Tower Q3 2025 US Android](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-android-top-5-arcade%20racing-revenue-us-60412396241bc16eb84404d2)
- Hill Climb Racing (Fingersoft; fully invented vehicles): 2B downloads for the original (Jan 2026) and 2.5B across the series (Dec 2024). HCR2 has $88.3M lifetime net IAP (AppMagic, late 2024). Fingersoft Oy's 2025 revenue was about €23.6M (Medium) — [Fingersoft](https://fingersoft.com/news/2026/01/15/hill-climb-racing-breaks-a-new-record-2-billion-downloads/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/fingersofts-hill-climb-racing-series-hits-25-billion-downloads); [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-hill-climb-racing-hits-2-5bn-mobiles-top-advertisers-2024s-top-ios-games-hybridcasual-and-south-korea-stats-more/)
- Racing Master (NetEase, Unreal): pitched as authentic high-end driving on mobile, with cross-platform play across PC and mobile (Medium; developer marketing) — [Unreal Engine interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/racing-master-brings-authentic-racing-action-and-best-in-class-graphics-to-mobile-and-other-platforms)
- Gran Velocita: a mobile sim with GT3, GT4, LMP, F4 and F1 classes. Its developer has announced a PC version and a Steam Early Access page, an indie heading toward mobile-to-PC like GridCorsa (Medium) — [GamingonPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/gran-velocita-brings-a-realistic-racing-sim-experience-with-real-car-classes-launching-this-month); [Game Developer press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/gran-velocita-real-driving-goes-pc); [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4910930)
- Assoluto Racing (mobile since May 2016) came to Switch on 16 Jul 2026 at $19.99 with 1-10 player online play. No download figures were found — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/games/assoluto-racing); [Deku Deals](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/assoluto-racing)
- CarX Drift Racing 3 is also in the mobile pipeline — [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/carx-drift-racing-3-hands-on)

### Inferences
- With Real Racing 3 gone, the "realistic circuit racing on phone" slot has no clear incumbent from a major Western publisher in 2026. Racing Master and Gran Velocita are the only active contenders found. That is GridCorsa's strongest positioning opening: real circuits, GT3 rules (pits, tyres, fuel, SR/licences) on phone, plus crossplay with PC.
- Real Racing 3's shutdown, which bricked purchases and gave no offline mode, is an emotional selling point. "Your progress and cars keep working offline" makes a credible pitch to ex-RR3 players.
- The CarX Street pattern (big mobile F2P base, then a $19.99 premium PC version) shows the mobile-to-PC route can work commercially for an unlicensed racer from a mid-size studio.
- A 1.6 GB APK is a friction point. Mobile racing buyers are used to big downloads (Asphalt, CarX), but casual users churn on install size.

### Gaps
- No worldwide 2025 revenue or download totals were found for CarX Street, Asphalt Legends, Real Racing 3 (its final years), Racing Master, Assoluto Racing, Rally Horizon or Mad Skills. They need AppMagic or Sensor Tower dashboards.
- Racing Master's current Western availability and whether its PC-mobile crossplay works in practice were not verified.
- Rally Horizon and Mad Skills Motocross were not researched (out of budget).

## 4. Common complaints about competitors that a new game could exploit

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints: (1) content costs too much, and DLC lands before the game is finished; (2) Early Access launches are thin and buggy, with poor optimisation and VR; (3) online lobbies are empty in the smaller sims; (4) entry hardware is expensive; (5) servers shut down and players lose what they bought. Each maps to a GridCorsa positioning claim.

### Cited Findings
- AC EVO: reviews called the content "quite lacking" for $39.99 and "a barebones, but promising start that currently isn't worth its price tag". Career mode was removed, VR was "unplayable" for many users, and high VRAM use made it unplayable on Steam Deck. Recent Steam reviews were "Mixed" (54% of 280 reviews in the last 30 days) against "Mostly Positive" lifetime. Its physics were praised (Medium) — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3058630/Assetto_Corsa_EVO); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/assetto-corsa-evo-unplayable-early-access-pc-vr-support/); [SteamDeckHQ](https://steamdeckhq.com/game-reviews/assetto-corsa-evo/); [Game8](https://game8.co/articles/reviews/assetto-corsa-evo-review-early-access); [Gamepressure (hidden online requirement for career)](https://gamepressure.com/newsroom/assetto-corsa-evo-draws-crowds-despite-rough-early-access-launch/z577be)
- LMU: review summaries list "aggressive monetization and DLC strategy". Paid DLC arrived "more ... quickly" than expected and before core modes and bug fixes. Early Access had "scant content and frequent bugs". ## 5. Unlicensed (brand-free) vs licensed racing games

### Takeaway
There is solid evidence that brand-free racing games can be commercial successes. BeamNG.drive (invented brands), Hill Climb Racing (2B+ downloads), CarX Street (50M+ downloads, top-10 Steam revenue at launch) and Wreckfest (about 1-2M Steam copies) are examples. But no source isolates licensing as a cause, and in the circuit-racing sim niche the leaders sell on licences (GT World Challenge, WEC, F1).

### Cited Findings
- BeamNG.drive uses invented makes and models (for example the "Ibishu Pigeon"). It averaged 20,660 concurrent on Steam in July 2026, close to Forza Horizon 6's about 29,000, and was #2 in US and #3 in EU PlayStation Store digital sales in Oct 2026 while still in Early Access. Its appeal is put down to soft-body physics and modding (Medium) — [OverTake (BeamNG fictional cars)](https://overtake.gg/news/comments/58590); [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/an-obscure-physics-sim-is-reeling-in-almost-as-many-players-as-forza-horizon-6-on-steam); [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/?p=109909)
- Wreckfest (Bugbear/THQ Nordic; fictional cars) made over $1M in one week of Early Access as Next Car Game. Steam estimates range from about 958K copies / $17.2M gross (Raijin) to about 2.13M / $38M (SteamData.ai), so third-party figures conflict (Low) — [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/?p=78855); [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/228380/Wreckfest); [SteamData.ai](https://steamdata.ai/game/228380)
- Hill Climb Racing (invented vehicles): 2B downloads for the original game and $88.3M lifetime IAP for HCR2 — [Fingersoft](https://fingersoft.com/news/2026/01/15/hill-climb-racing-breaks-a-new-record-2-billion-downloads/); [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-hill-climb-racing-hits-2-5bn-mobiles-top-advertisers-2024s-top-ios-games-hybridcasual-and-south-korea-stats-more/)
- CarX Street has no real car brands (lookalike cars) and reached 50M+ mobile downloads, $10M+ mobile revenue in a year, and a top-10 Steam revenue launch at $19.99 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-45242)
- Licence-led counterpoints: ACC is the official GT World Challenge game, LMU the official WEC/ELMS game, F1 25 the official F1 game, and RaceRoom markets DTM and WTCR — [Traxion LMU ELMS](https://racinggames.gg/article/le-mans-ultimates-elms-dlc-silverstone-lmp3s-and-major-updates-arrive); [RaceRoom](https://racinggames.gg/article/raceroom-summer-campaign-2025-free-content-huge-sales-game-changing-update-detailed)

### Inferences
- In sandbox, arcade and mobile racing, brand-free games are a proven path, and players accept lookalike cars if the handling and customisation are good. For a "GT3 sim" pitch, the lack of real brands mainly costs credibility with hardcore buyers who want specific cars (for example "the 992 GT3 R"). It costs much less with gamepad and phone players.
- Deep paint, number and rim customisation is a sensible substitute for brands. CarX's tuning and customisation is a core loop, and BeamNG's community builds identity around invented makes.
- Keeping real circuit names, which GridCorsa does, keeps much of the authenticity signal at lower licensing risk. Whether those names need permission was not researched here.

### Gaps
- No study or postmortem directly comparing sales of licensed and unlicensed racers was found. Evidence is case-based.
- The trademark and legal position of using real circuit names without a licence was not researched.

## 6. Browser/web racing game market (CrazyGames, Poki) and fit

### Takeaway
Web portals have very large free audiences: Poki claims 100M+ MAU and CrazyGames claims 50-60M. Revenue is ad-funded and sessions are short (most commonly 11-20 minutes). That suits a light, quick-race funnel but not a 1.6 GB, long-race sim. A web version works best as a top-of-funnel demo pushing to the app, since 62% of web gamers say they've bought or downloaded a game after discovering it on the web.

### Cited Findings
- Poki: 100M+ MAU in 2026 (10M in 2020), 1B gameplays a month, 600+ developer partners. It reported welcoming 625M players in 2025 (High, company claims) — [Poki/Atomik 2026 State of Web Gaming report](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-06-25-the_2026_state_of_web_gaming_report_4ee6fdc260_wp/); [DigitalMediaNet](https://digitalmedianet.com/poki-announces-milestone-of-625-million-players-without-raising-external-funding/amp/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/inside-pokis-vision-for-the-future-of-browser-gaming/)
- CrazyGames: a company year-end 2025 post said 50M MAU. Other materials claim 60M+, and a Semrush-based third-party estimate is about 35M MAU and about 300M gameplays a month (Low/Medium; definitions differ) — [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/crazygames-vs-poki-vs-coolmath-games-2026/); [LinkedIn (CrazyGames staff 2025 recap)](https://tr.linkedin.com/in/yagizefemertol)
- Web gamer behaviour (US and UK, n=2,000, May 2026): 37% play several times a day. The most common session is 11-20 minutes (29%), 22% play 21-30 minutes and 15% play more than an hour. 58% play because the games are free. Web games are "almost entirely funded by ads". 62% have bought or downloaded a game after discovering it on the web (72% of the most frequent players). Among developers, 53% plan to port mobile games to the web within 12 months and 31% fear cannibalisation — [Poki 2026 State of Web Gaming](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-06-25-the_2026_state_of_web_gaming_report_4ee6fdc260_wp/)
- Developers say the real work of web porting is cutting load size and adapting controls — same source.
- Hill Climb Racing is cited as a successful web title (no data given) — same source.

### Inferences
- GridCorsa's existing Three.js browser version could serve as a lightweight "quick race / hotlap" funnel on portals, with a deep link to the Android/PC app. Portals usually require small initial loads and ad-SDK integration, which would need checking.
- The web's ad-funded, short-session economics conflict with long races, pit strategy and the coins IAP model, so the web should be a discovery channel rather than the main revenue source.

### Gaps
- No public figures were found for racing or driving's share of plays or revenue on Poki or CrazyGames, or for typical racing game RPMs or eCPMs.
- Portal requirements (size limits, SDKs, exclusivity) were not researched.
