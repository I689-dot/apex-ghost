# Engine recordings

The `engine_*.bin` loop banks are built with `tools/asset-pipeline/engine_bank.py` from recordings by
**Pole Position Production** (https://www.polepositionproduction.com), distributed in the free Sonniss GDC
Game Audio Bundles (https://gdc.sonniss.com). The bundle licence allows royalty-free use in commercial and
non-commercial projects; attribution is not required, but given here with thanks.

| bank | source recording |
|---|---|
| engine_flat6 | Porsche 997 Cup 2012 – t4 – Onboard, Engine, Right (GDC 2020) |
| engine_v10 | Audi R8 2017 – t3 – Onboard, Engine, Right (GDC 2020) |
| engine_i6 | BMW M4 2014 – t4 – Onboard, Drive, Gearshifts, Engine Center (GDC 2020) |
| engine_v8tt | McLaren 570S 2016 – t10 – Onboard, Fast, Exhaust Right (GDC 2020) |
| engine_v8f | Ferrari 488 2016 – t9 – Onboard, Drive, Steady RPMs, Exhaust Left (GDC 2020) |
| engine_viper | Dodge Viper GTS V10 Race Engine 2014 – t4 – Onboard, Ramps, Exhaust Left (GDC 2021-2023) |
